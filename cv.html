Research

Who Chooses Your Hospital? Inherited Demand and the Measurement of Hospital Competition

Job Market Paper

Shows that elective surgical demand is routed by referral menus belonging to primary care organizations rather than chosen by patients, using claims covering 8.5 million commercially insured Californians. Estimates a consideration-set demand model placing 2.4 of 48 available hospitals on the effective menu; replaying observed organization closures, the standard merger-review model recovers 41% of measured within-system diversion against 88% for the menu model.

Heterogeneous Willingness-to-Pay for Electric Vehicles

3rd Year Paper

Merged U.S. auto sales with detailed vehicle attributes; estimated a random-coefficients logit (BLP) with demographic interactions and micro-moments. Recovered WTP for battery capacity and range; quantified price sensitivity across household segments.

Road Infrastructure & Agricultural Outcomes

Masters Thesis

Built a county-level panel dataset linking village road access to agricultural land rents using newly compiled data. Produced spatial analyses and maps in ArcGIS to assess how improved road connectivity affected local land values and farming outcomes.
