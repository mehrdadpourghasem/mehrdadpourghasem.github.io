Work in Progress

Who Chooses Your Hospital? Primary-Care Referrals and Hospital Merger Review

Hospital merger review relies on demand models in which patients choose among all nearby hospitals. In HMOs and other gatekeeper plans, however, patients reach a hospital through referrals that begin at their primary-care practice, and each practice sends its patients to a small set of hospitals. Linking 184 million claims from California's all-payer claims database (2018–2023) to federal provider registries, I reconstruct the sequence of care that leads to each patient's admission and re-estimate the willingness-to-pay model used in merger review, restricting each patient's choice set to the hospitals her practice routinely sends patients to. In gatekeeper plans the referral-based model clears more than a quarter of the mergers the standard model flags; in open-access plans the two predict nearly the same diversion.
