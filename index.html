Work in Progress

Who Chooses Your Hospital? Inherited Demand and the Measurement of Hospital Competition

Hospital merger review rests on demand models in which patients choose hospitals. Using claims covering 8.5 million commercially insured Californians, I show elective surgical demand is routed by referral menus belonging to primary care organizations, not chosen by patients. Replaying observed organization closures, the standard model recovers 41% of measured within-system diversion against 88% for a consideration-set menu model — a bias large enough to clear mergers that should be flagged.
